My Values

I am a regulated healthcare professional with two university degrees who has access to the controlled act of psychotherapy in Ontario. I am also a mom who does the best that I can.

As a therapist I value:

Compassion first.

I am not doing my job if you don't feel support, care, and love.

Learning from my clients.

You teach me about life as much as I teach you. And for that I am grateful.

Fun.

Therapy for kids should always be fun.

Personalized, committed therapy.

I only take 10 clients per week to devote my full attention and energy to the families that I serve. I make myself available during work hours to support you via email. You can expect me to respond to email Monday to Wednesday from 9 to 5pm.

A mother's perspective.

I use my experience as a new mother to inform my clinical practice. I have been humbled by how challenging it is to raise a child. My hope is that I can use my perspective as a mother to show more compassion and understanding to my clients.

As a person I value:

  • Unwavering devotion to my family.
  • Learning from other cultures around the globe.
  • Respecting the multiculturalism of this city.
  • Trauma-informed yoga.
  • Friendship.
  • Taking care of our beautiful planet.
  • Standing up for what is right.

Intentional, Sustainable Travel

I value intentional, sustainable travel that simultaneously respects the country that I am visiting while serving as a medium to teach me more about myself and my therapeutic practice. The privilege that allows me to travel informs my lens within the multicultural Toronto community that I serve.
