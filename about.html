About Me

I grew up with a neurodiverse brain, and still experience what it’s like to live with what others call a ‘disability’. Although at many points in my life it has been challenging to work around my learning disabilities, sensory processing differences, and anxiety, I am beyond grateful since this personal experience fueled my dedication to help kids, who like me, just need a bit of extra encouragement, support, and love to reach their fullest potential.

Why I Became an OT

When I was in elementary school, I promised myself that one day when I grew up, I would dedicate my career to helping kids so they felt less alone - no matter what it took. I began my education at the University of Toronto while spending over five years as a volunteer at Holland Bloorview Kids Rehabilitation Hospital. From there, after completing my Master of Science in Occupational Therapy at McMaster University, I knew I wanted to specialize in pediatrics. As a 30 something year old adult who has spent countless hours in therapy growing up, I know that the right support at the right time can be transformative in a child's functioning, self-esteem, and participation in daily life.

My Promise to Families

Every child deserves to participate in what matters to them. Every child develops at their own pace. I respect that process. I view challenges not as deficits but as different ways of experiencing the world. Your child will be celebrated while under my care. Whether it's learning to write their name, enjoying mealtimes for the first time, or playing confidently with friends, I'm here to help your child thrive.

Why I Love What I Do

I deeply value the relationships I build with families. Parents are the true experts on their children, and I consider myself privileged to support families on their journey. Together, we celebrate successes, navigate challenges, and work toward meaningful goals that enhance your child's quality of life.

In My Free Time (Professionally)

I have the privilege of being an Assistant Clinical Professor (Adjunct) at McMaster University with a focus on teaching future occupational therapists in the pediatric term. To give back to my community, I am a volunteer therapist at Family Service Toronto and at the Early On Centres across the Greater Toronto Area.

In My Free Time (Personally)

I love being a mother and a wife. I love playing complex 5-hour long board games. I love working on my novel. I value traveling to the farthest countries that I can reach such as India, Indonesia, and New Zealand. I love hiking 20km in one day in a bush somewhere. Pilates and dance are my happy places.
