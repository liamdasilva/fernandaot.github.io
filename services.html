Comprehensive Occupational Therapy Assessment

In-depth evaluation of your child's strengths, needs, and daily environments

Total Cost $500–$700

Areas Assessed

  • Sensory Processing
  • Emotional Regulation
  • Feeding difficulties
  • Play and Engagement
  • All motor skills (gross motor, fine motor)
  • Activities of daily living such as sleep and toileting
  • School skills
  • Childhood anxiety

Four-Part Process

  1. One-hour virtual parent appointment to get to know you, your family, and your child's needs $200
  2. One-hour in-home assessment with you and your child $200
  3. A full 8-10 page report (or a 1-page summary report at no cost if preferred) $200 / $0
  4. Thirty-minute virtual meeting to discuss the assessment results $100

Treatment Fee

Individualized ongoing therapy and collaborative support for your child

Hourly Rate $200 / hour

What This Fee Applies To

  • OT Treatment virtual or in person in your home or location of choice
  • Consultation with Parents
  • Consultation with Care Providers
  • Consultation with School Staff

Included at No Additional Cost

While you and your family are under my care, I offer the following at no extra charge:

  • Email support (typical response within 24–48 hours)
  • School accommodation letters
  • OAP letter writing

Accessible Care & Sliding-Scale Rates

Every family deserves a safe space to navigate life’s challenges. My private practice is built on a commitment to accessible care. To help make assessment and therapy more affordable, I reserve a portion of my practice for sliding-scale rates based on income and financial need. I am committed to fighting back against financial barriers so as many families as possible can access quality occupational therapy care.

Please reach out to discuss your specific needs as a family and we can go from there.
